# SCI-003 — Evidence-Based Research Dossier and Editorial Brief

Base: PR8 was verified merged into master (`8324d04`). This slice preserves Source/Story/metadata aliases, existing dossiers/claims/evidence/claim_evidence/editorial_decisions, Auth/RLS, queue/leases, production ledger, HLD and release gates. Implementation and synthetic regression checks are separate from the real-paper human acceptance run. **The real dossier is prepared; its human review/approval must be performed by the user, not manufactured by the coding agent.** Until persisted review and whole-dossier approval are present, its brief is preliminary and the scientific gate is blocked.

## Primary document intake

`/editorial/research` supports a verified SCI-002 metadata snapshot linked to an existing Story/Source, exact version, canonical source URL, access basis, reuse-license status/URL and attribution. Text is limited to 256 KiB; its SHA-256 is computed by PostgreSQL. Original text is stored privately in Academy. A pasted text has a **text segment** locator, never a fictitious PDF page number. `abstract_only` intake must exactly match the primary snapshot abstract; its scope follows the dossier/brief and adds a mandatory methods/full-paper limitation. User declarations about scope and document identity still require human inspection; a matching bibliography cannot prove an uploaded PDF is the correct paper.

Human-uploaded PDF uses narrow authenticated Storage policies under `research-intake/{actor}/{uuid}.pdf` in the existing private bucket. Only editor can upload; members may read registered originals through user-session signed URLs. No public bucket, browser service-role key or arbitrary URL download. `request_primary_pdf` records the verified session actor and queues the existing worker. A live job lease and still-active editor are required at completion. Worker enforces 4 MiB, 40 pages, 256 KiB extracted text, a 20-second isolated parser timeout and 128 MiB old-generation heap limit; OCR/password-protected/scanned/malformed documents fail closed. Parser runs in a worker thread, disables eval/font execution, reads bytes only and performs no network or embedded document-action execution. Raw bytes and extracted-page content have separate SHA-256 bindings. Text-layer extraction can have ordering/hyphenation/math errors; reviewers inspect the original PDF and persisted page side by side.

The local operator CLI accepts an explicitly supplied PDF and curated structured dossier. It uses trusted worker privileges, preserves `author_type=agent`, leaves human identity null, uploads without overwriting an existing checksum path, validates the bytes and records zero provider charge with the atomic ledger. It does not impersonate the editor or create human decisions. The web intake remains available for human-provided PDF/text. Unknown reuse permission is preserved; access, short quotation and permission to republish figures are distinct.

## Evidence and scientific meaning

Each evidence record reuses `academy.evidence`, links Source/document/dossier/version, exact short quote, PDF page or text-segment locator, scientific context, supplied numeric results, uncertainty/limitations, extraction method and `exact_quote_matched`. SQL checks the quote against the actual stored page; invented quotations and page numbers cannot be saved through the command. This is mechanical extraction accuracy, not semantic or independent scientific verification. Evidence and claim/evidence links are immutable.

Claims reuse `academy.claims`: measured result (observation/experiment), author interpretation, added `theoretical_prediction`, editorial explanation or speculation. The legacy vendor_claim type remains compatible. Domain-pack evidence levels are checked against the Story's domain, not a generic confidence score. Claims without evidence remain explicitly `unsupported`. Claims with matching passages remain `quote_matched`, not automatically supported in meaning. A misleading interpretation can cite a real quote and still require rejection by a human.

`claims.status` remains **unverified** after this source-meaning review; no automatic independent verification, replication or publication. Existing article/video gates continue to block these claims. Any future verifier integration requires its own evaluation and metered budget decision.

## Immutable dossier revisions

Existing `dossiers` now binds primary document, full draft hash, author type/identity and idempotent command. The draft contains thirteen required sections: question, background, methodology, findings, quantitative results, supporting evidence, limitations, alternatives, demonstrated result, unknowns, significance, questions and references. Statements identify claim keys and provenance: source-supported, author interpretation, AI explanation, unknown or disputed. A source-supported statement cannot be unmapped.

Separate editorial planning holds angle, audience, why it matters, novelty, headline, background explanations, concepts, visuals and open questions. These are **proposals**, not approved facts. Both planning and the scientific sections are visible to the whole-dossier reviewer. The web editor offers fields for sections/evidence/claims/planning and creates a new revision; it never overwrites a document, evidence, claim or previous dossier. Changing document/evidence creates a new input/revision and requires new review. Old published revisions remain untouched.

## Human scientific review and approval

Existing membership boundaries stay intact for selection, imports, budgets and document/dossier edits. SCI-003 gives editor/reviewer a narrowly scoped scientific-review command; reviewer still cannot edit/intake/generate a brief or change other state. The human cannot review/approve their own authored dossier. Trusted worker-only agent drafts can be reviewed by the human editor. Worker cannot execute human review/approval RPCs.

Per claim: accept, narrow, reject or request revision, with reviewer UUID, reason, timestamp, exact dossier hash/version, previous review/version, idempotent command and explicitly self-reported effort seconds. Narrow preserves a specific replacement wording. Unsupported accept/narrow is rejected; a source-contradicting interpretation requires human rejection. Review replay is exact; stale or conflicting commands fail.

After every claim has a final decision, at least one is accepted/narrowed and no request_revision remains, a **separate whole-dossier approval** is required. It reuses persisted `editorial_decisions`, policy `scientific-dossier/1`, binds dossier ID/hash and the exact current review IDs, and covers all sections and editorial framing. A new claim review, new dossier or new primary-document intake invalidates readiness without mutating historical records. This approval never writes article/video approvals or publishes a Story. A newer document is detected by intake sequence even inside one transaction. A known abstract cannot be labelled full text, a narrowing requires nonempty replacement wording, and asserted dossier narrative cannot retain rejected claim references.

## Editorial Brief and downstream contract

`create_editorial_brief` either creates a clearly labelled **PRELIMINARY** package or requires current per-claim review and whole-dossier approval. Final approvedFindings are generated only from accepted/narrowed persisted decisions; rejected and unsupported claims are excluded. Narrowed wording, evidence UUIDs, review UUIDs, dossier/document IDs/version/checksum and metadata reference are retained. Mandatory caveats include claim caveats, abstract/full-text scope, absence of independent verification and separate media rights. All requested planning fields are copied from the reviewed exact dossier, never silently invented at brief time.

Briefs are immutable. A historical brief can become stale when new reviews/inputs arrive; consumers must recheck the current research gate and review-ID set. A brief is not an ES/EN article, Spanish script or publication approval. No full articles/videos are generated in this slice.

## Real-paper demonstration

Existing local Source: [Kozma and Hughes, arXiv:2610.30098v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30098v1), *Pure dephasing destroys two-photon amplitude, not nonlinear phase*. Its linked [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) was inspected; the eight-page PDF was explicitly operator-selected, not crawled. SHA-256: `f14feae673fbebf1b7c75463f37a38756fa75722b079db9f294db9afb6344ad3`. Source pages 1 and 3 were rendered/visually checked against text extraction. Do not generalize the paper's calculated phase result to practical device immunity: the singular reference, pulse/model assumptions and coherent-amplitude penalty matter.

The persisted agent dossier has 5 claims, 4 with literal evidence links, 1 without evidence, and 3 located passages. Two deliberately false controls require rejection: an unsupported commercial/experimental improvement and an inference of practical dephasing immunity despite a matching quote. The source is theoretical; the local artifact does not claim a new experiment or independent verification. Input and attribution are in `examples/research/`; full original and reports stay in ignored `artifacts/research-pilot` / private Storage.

`research:local:prepare` settled the local intake/dossier tool at **$0 provider charge**, approximately 3.35 seconds for local parse/persistence in this run (metadata refresh and human review are additional). Human effort and complete workflow duration remain unknown until a real review is recorded. Do not replace missing human metrics with synthetic test timings. The real review record and reviewed Brief are pending user action; a preliminary planning artifact has no approved findings.
