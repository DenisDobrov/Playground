## Implementation contract

- Public release DTOs remain the only article/image source, fetched anonymously. Drafts and campaign images do not become public through SEO routes.
- Explicit absolute 1200×630 share images, title/description, large Twitter cards and reciprocal ES/EN/x-default links. Article images are typographic cards built from the exact released headline, not new scientific illustrations.
- Escaped `NewsArticle` JSON-LD includes canonical URL, language, original sources and the visible Dobrov Academy organizational byline. No invented scientific author, independent verification or review rating.
- Initial publication date remains the first release. Modified date changes only when the public content or slug changes. Campaign-only reapproval preserves both dates. Stored releases are never rewritten. A bounded 1,000-row history query fails explicitly on truncation rather than inventing dates; pagination must precede that scale.
- Homepages become a compact article directory; weekly editions retain their complete excerpts and caveats. A public WhatsApp follow link uses the existing channel. No new substantive news copy or approval form.
- Sitemap adds both homepages and editorial-method pages: 40 URLs for the existing 34 articles. Private routes, demo stories, images and drafts are omitted. No claim that markup guarantees ranking.

## Analytics privacy and cost contract

Pinned official `@vercel/analytics` 2.0.1. Server enablement requires Vercel **production** and live mode. Only actual released article/digest components and public method pages mount analytics; previews/local/demo do not collect.

Explicit route/path disables automatic browser-history collection. A fail-closed `beforeSend` allows only the current mounted public path on dobrov.org/www.dobrov.org, removes query strings/fragments, rejects custom events and private navigation, respects DNT/GPC, and drops visits with potentially sensitive referrer query/fragment/private path. No session extraction, identity properties or form values. A global strict-origin referrer policy prevents full public URLs being forwarded; the existing private no-referrer policy still wins. ES/EN method pages describe analytics and distinguish necessary login cookies.

Hobby measurement is public pageviews, aggregate referrers/countries/devices. UTM campaign reports and custom conversion events are not available on this plan. Native channel metrics plus article/referrer counts are the initial measurement; pageviews do not establish completed reads or follower conversions. At the free cap, ingestion stops rather than purchasing an overage. Do not upgrade automatically.

Primary references checked 7 November 2026: [Vercel limits and pricing](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/limits-and-pricing), [package and beforeSend](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/package), [privacy](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy), [Google Article markup](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article), [sitemaps](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap).
